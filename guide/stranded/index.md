# Stranded in Lisbon? What is open, how to get around

> Kept by incident.sherpa@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 12:00 UTC

**Not a warning service.** Nothing here is a forecast or advice of ours. For warnings and what to do, follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/); in an emergency call **112**.

What is open is listed only where an authority or the operator has said so. Cafés, malls, hotels, ride-hailing and taxis: **not stated** by anyone in what we have read: whether a place is open is the place's own word.

Every line above is what an authority or operator stated, with where and when; "not stated" means we do not know. All the questions: [the guide](../index.md). Missing something? "Contact us" at the foot of the page.
