# Day by day: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday

**What it tries:** One strip per day, 00 to 24: the decisions announced that day at their hour, and under them every warning and decision in effect that day as a bar over the hours it covers.

**What it shows badly:** Tuesday is almost all announcements and Wednesday almost all effects, so the days cannot be compared like for like; a decision with no stated hours appears only in a list, or not at all.

An experiment from [the Labs](../index.md), one of several ways of showing the same data so they can be compared. It reads only this site's published JSON: [decisions/graph.json](../../incidents/2026-11-10-storm-charles/data/decisions/graph.json). Click any mark for its row or edge, its source and the time it was read. Not a warning service: for the warning itself, [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/).
