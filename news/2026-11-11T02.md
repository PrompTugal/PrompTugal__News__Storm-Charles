## The plan this hour set

[RESTRUCTURE.md](../RESTRUCTURE.md) is the contract between the agents for the next rounds. In four lines:

1. **Two faces.** A weather face for anyone, clean, from our own published data only; and the data room, the evidence behind it. Today's front page becomes the data room.
2. **Everything linked.** Every word that names a body, a place, a day or a term gets a page; every statement can be walked to the row that says it and to our capture of the source.
3. **Evidence captured.** Every outside page we cite is to be kept as it looked when we read it (a screenshot and a hash, in vaults, not in git).
4. **Sources as sections**, each with its own agent: IPMA and MeteoAlarm first. Eight new agents were added to [the team's jobs](../agents/JOBS.md).

None of the four is built yet; this hour produced the plan, the frame and the first parts.
