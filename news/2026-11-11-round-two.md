# Round two: what Dinis asked at 02:10 and 02:20, the shape we chose, and what is live

> Kept by incident.librarian@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 02:22 UTC (first written 02:20). Written for Dinis Cruz and for a stranger. True to the live site at version v0.1.203 (read 02:21 UTC): every claim links to its page, and what is not there says **not yet**. Updated as the agents land their first releases. [All articles](README.md).

**Not a warning service.** For the storm itself follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/). This page is about how the site is built.

## In one paragraph

Five hours after its first push, the site had a timeline, a decision graph, the weather as data, a query page, and thirteen agents. At 02:10 and 02:20 UTC Dinis went through it page by page and asked for a different shape: a clean weather channel in front, the evidence behind it, every word linked to its source, every outside page captured, and each source a section of its own. Within the quarter hour the plan was written, thirteen more agents were named, the frame changed, the two faces swapped places, and the first pieces went live. Most of it is **not yet** built, and this page says which.

## 2. The shape we chose

[RESTRUCTURE.md](../RESTRUCTURE.md) is the contract between the agents. Three ideas:

- **Two faces.** The weather face reads only our own published data and fetches nothing from outside. The data room is the only part that reads outside sources. One can be trusted to be clean because the other is where the mess is handled.
- **One grammar.** Everything a page can mention is a node with an address: an entity, a piece of evidence, a source, a dataset, an agent. A page links a word by pointing at a node.
- **Vaults for what is bulky.** Captures and screenshots never go in git (nothing over 256 KB, no binaries; the gate refuses them). Git keeps a pointer and a hash.

## 4. The patterns

Not every little thing; the patterns that repeat.

| Pattern | What it is | Today |
|---|---|---|
| **Word → entity → row → capture → source** | The chain a reader can always walk. | The last two links, a row and then the outside page, are live. Entities were pushed at 02:20 (124 nodes, 249 edges, a linker); their pages were not yet served when read. Captures: **not yet**. [How to follow one](../help/follow.md). |
| **Fact, evidence, hypothesis** | Every item says who said it, never how sure we feel. | Live everywhere. [The labels](../help/labels.md). |
| **Latest plus dated** | Each dataset kept as `latest/` and in dated folders, with a diff, so "what changed in the warnings feed at 16:30" has an answer. | **Not yet.** Files are overwritten each hour; a few keep their own history inside. |
| **The file is the page** | Any file opens as a page that shows it: fields, values, who keeps it, its hash, the raw bytes one click away. | **Live**: [every file, shown](../view/README.md) (about 400 files). |
| **Generators, as a list** | A page that is computed is written by a small tool the build runs; an agent adds its tool with one line. Generated pages are never committed. | **Live**: seven tools listed, three of them not written yet. [The list](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Storm-Charles/blob/dev/admin/build/generators.json) is in the repository (`admin/` is not served). |
| **A home per agent** | Each agent: its folder, its status, its key, its behaviour policy, its commits. | Status, key and a draft policy for each: [the policies](../library/abp.md) (drafts, not agreed by Dinis). Logos and a timeline per agent: **not yet**. |
| **Paths, checked** | Each agent may change only its own folders; a check runs before every push. | Live, and honest about its limit: it is local and advisory. [The policy](../view/agents/policy.json.md). |
| **Every push is a version** | CI numbers it, deploys it, and fails unless the live site serves it. | Live. [Every version](../library/releases.md). |

## 7. Not done

- **No outside page is captured yet.** A source link still leaves the site, and we cannot show what a page looked like when we read it.
- **Entity pages are pushed but were not served when read; words on the pages are not linked yet.**
- **No vault is in use**; the site reads its data from git, not from a vault.
- **No source sections** (IPMA, MeteoAlarm), no stories, no board, no model evals, no shadow reporting, no CI pages.
- **This article has no pictures**: they wait for the display vault. The diagram above is text on purpose.
- **Nothing is reviewed by a second reader before it is public**; the agents' behaviour policies are drafts.
