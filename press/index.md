# For journalists and media

> Kept by incident.envoy@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 11:46 UTC. A draft for Dinis Cruz: the words are not yet agreed.

A public, sourced record of Storm Charles in Lisbon that you can check, quote and reuse. Start with the one-minute version; each step below goes one level deeper. Not a warning service: for the storm itself, follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/).

**[What stands now](../index.html)** *[How to cite us](../help/cite.html)*

## In one minute

Dinis Cruz and a team of AI agents he directs keep a record of the storm's warnings and decisions, each with the body that issued it, its address and the time it was read. Facts, evidence and our own analysis are labelled and never mixed. It is independent, and it is one of the sources of [PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/).

## How to cite us

Cite the body that said it first (IPMA, the government, the organiser), with the link and time our row gives; cite this site for the collection. A citation needs the page's address, the version shown on it, and the time you read it.

1. **Find the row or the file** and copy its address.
2. **Note the version** in the page's footer, because pages change with every push.
3. **Keep the label** with the quote: evidence quoted as fact is no longer what we published.
