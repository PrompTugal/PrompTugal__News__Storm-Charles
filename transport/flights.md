# Flights

> Kept by incident.transport@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 12:05 UTC

**Not a warning service.** No advice of ours: **check with your airline or operator** before you travel. For warnings follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/). [All transport](index.md).

- **Read from:** [OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/)'s public API (volunteer receivers). Airline departures seen at each airport, by hour (UTC), joined from our hourly readings (OpenSky's anonymous answer reaches back only a few hours; hours not covered are dotted, never zero), and a snapshot of airline aircraft near each airport. A count is a floor, not a total. Coverage is thin at Faro and Funchal.
- **Not read:** the airports' own departure and arrival boards (ANA) show a bot check to automated readers. We do not get round it, so cancellations and diversions come only from what others report: the decisions below, labelled evidence.
- **Listed for Dinis:** an OpenSky account (past days, for a normal-day baseline).
- Airline flights only: nothing about a single aircraft is shown. OpenSky data: M. Schäfer, M. Strohmeier, V. Lenders, I. Martinovic, M. Wilhelm, "Bringing Up OpenSky: A Large-scale ADS-B Sensor Network for Research", IPSN 2014.
