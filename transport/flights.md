# Flights

> Kept by incident.transport@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 13:46 UTC

**Not a warning service.** No advice of ours. **Your airline or operator decides whether your flight or service runs: its site or app has the latest.** For warnings follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/). [All transport](index.md).

- **Read from:** [OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/)'s public API (volunteer receivers). Airline departures seen at each airport, by hour (UTC), joined from our hourly readings (OpenSky's anonymous answer reaches back only a few hours; hours not covered are dotted, never zero), and a snapshot of airline aircraft near each airport. A count is a floor, not a total. Coverage is thin at Faro and Funchal.
- **ANA's own figures:** the airport operator's "update by airport" statements in [its newsroom](https://en.newsroom.ana.pt/): movements scheduled, cancelled in advance, cancelled during the day and operated, per airport, at the time ANA states (read hourly from 11 November, 13:44 UTC). Numbers only, with the link: ANA's terms ask consent to reproduce its text.
- **Not read:** the airports' own departure and arrival boards (ANA) show a bot check to automated readers, and we do not get round it. Diversions come only from what others report (the researcher's decisions, below).
- **Listed for Dinis:** an OpenSky account (past days, for a normal-day baseline).
- Airline flights only: nothing about a single aircraft is shown. OpenSky data: M. Schäfer, M. Strohmeier, V. Lenders, I. Martinovic, M. Wilhelm, "Bringing Up OpenSky: A Large-scale ADS-B Sensor Network for Research", IPSN 2014.
