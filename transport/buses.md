# Buses and trams

> Kept by incident.transport@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 12:55 UTC

**Not a warning service.** No advice of ours. **Your airline or operator decides whether your flight or service runs: its site or app has the latest.** For warnings follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/). [All transport](index.md).

- **Carris Metropolitana:** its open API: every notice in force (`/v2/alerts`), how many are for the weather, short excerpts; and the buses running now, on how many lines (`/v2/vehicles`), hour by hour from our readings. Its network data is CC BY on dados.gov.pt.
- **Carris** (Lisbon's buses, trams and lifts): its [terms](https://www.carris.pt/a-carris/termos-e-condicoes/) (clause 2.2) say no content of its site may be published without its written consent, and it has no open feed, so we do not read it into our pages. Its own [service changes](https://www.carris.pt/viaje/alteracoes-de-servico/) page has the latest (terms read 11 November, 12:48 UTC).
- **STCP (Porto):** how many buses report a position now and on how many lines, from its real-time feed on [dados.gov.pt](https://dados.gov.pt/datasets/urban-platform-bus-location/) (CC0). Counts only.
