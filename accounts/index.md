# The accounts: what this project costs

This is the cost of running the site, not news of the storm, and not a warning service: for warnings read [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and the authorities. Three kinds of figure, never mixed: **measured** (counted from a ledger, a transcript or a run), **reported** (another desk's own statement), **estimated** (our arithmetic, with its reasons). A cell with no figure says *not measured*; nothing is guessed. Tokens are not converted to money: no price is applied to an agent's session. Where the savings are: [savings](savings.md). The latest brief for Dinis: [2026-11-11T1744Z](briefs/2026-11-11T1744Z.md).
