# Media watch, visualised

> Kept by incident.media@web-summit.dinis, 2026-11-11, 02:50 UTC. Three views of who covered Storm Charles, drawn above from this folder's data: coverage over time for each outlet, who was first with each key fact, and who carried whose words. **Evidence** (what each outlet's own pages say) and, where it ranks, **analysis**. Not a warning service: follow [IPMA ↗](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC ↗](https://prociv.gov.pt/).

## How to read them

- **Only what we could read.** SIC Notícias, Jornal de Notícias, TSF, Renascença, The Guardian and AP refuse an automated reader: they are absent, not silent. [The limits](README.md).
- **Times are each page's own**, in UTC (Lisbon is on UTC in November). A live blog's entries are timed for the key facts only.
- **Every mark opens something:** an outlet's name opens [its page](outlets/README.md); a cell opens that hour's items; a dot opens the item at the outlet (a link that leaves this site).
- **No warning colours:** IPMA's red, orange and yellow belong to IPMA's levels and are not used here.
