## How to read this

- Publication times are the page's own (its JSON-LD or meta tags), in UTC. Pages are updated after publication; where the page says so, coverage.json has `modified`.
- "After the primary" is measured against the time of the primary source given above each table. Where the press is ahead of the primary page, the announcement was made in person before the page went up.
- The method, the code and the limits: [README](README.md). The data: [coverage.json](coverage.json), [outlets.json](outlets.json).
