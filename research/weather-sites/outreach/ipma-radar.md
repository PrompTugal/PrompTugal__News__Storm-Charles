# Draft: to IPMA, about showing the radar image

**Status: draft, not sent. A person sends it, in their own name.** Where to send: IPMA's own contact page ("Contacto" on ipma.pt); no address is kept here. Before sending, Dinis settles the two points in square brackets.

## What it rests on

IPMA's legal notice (https://www.ipma.pt/pt/siteinfo/index.html?page=index.xml, read 10 November 2026, 23:53 UTC) allows free use "para uso pessoal ou público desde que dessa utilização não decorram finalidades lucrativas ou ofensivas", with the source always named and, on web pages, IPMA's logo and a link to its site. It does not say whether an image may be loaded from IPMA's server inside another site's page. The radar page is marked "copyright © IPMA".

I write on behalf of storm-charles.promptugal.news, a [non-profit: to be confirmed by the sender] page that brings together, for people in Lisbon during storm Charles, IPMA's warnings, the decisions of authorities and event organisers, and their sources, each item with a link and the time read.
