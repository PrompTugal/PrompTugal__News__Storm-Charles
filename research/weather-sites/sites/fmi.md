## What it shows, and how

Local forecast with an hourly table and a 5-day warnings strip per region; open data (observations, radar, HARMONIE, lightning) also on a public cloud bucket.

Forms: `timeline-hourly`, `warnings`, `radar`, `api`.

## What it does best

The warnings strip: five days across, one row per hazard.

## What we take from it

A five-day strip with one row per hazard is the compact form of IPMA's warnings.
