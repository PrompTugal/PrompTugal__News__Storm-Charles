## What it shows, and how

A map of civil-protection occurrences in near real time, built on ANEPC's data, with an API; made for fires, used in storms for falls of trees, floods and structures.

Forms: `maps`, `tracker`, `api`.

## What it does best

Occurrences as open data, by volunteers.

## What we take from it

A count of occurrences by hour is the "impact so far" figure, sourced to ANEPC via this API.
