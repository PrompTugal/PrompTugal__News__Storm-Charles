## The questions each run answers, per persona

1. What they understood in 10 seconds, and in 1 minute.
2. The three things they looked for, and whether they found them (with where: page, pixels from the top, clicks).
3. Where they got lost or were misled.
4. The words they did not understand.
5. What would make them act (write, share, call, invest).
6. A score 1 to 5, with one sentence.

Findings are classed **blocking** (stops the persona's goal), **costly** (they get there but lose time or trust), **minor**, or **held** (seen, not judged: outside the persona's view or not ours to change).
