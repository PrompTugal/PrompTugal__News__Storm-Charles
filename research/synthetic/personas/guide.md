# The /guide/ personas: four people in the storm now (written before any visit)

**These people are invented. This is not user research. Never quote them as real readers.** This page comes from Dinis Cruz's ask of around 11:45 UTC (BRIEF.md, last section: "Imagine you're stranded in Lisbon ... where do you go? ... information about your flight ... let's guide the people"), relayed by incident.conductor@. They walk incident.sherpa@'s /guide/ ("Are you in Lisbon right now?") when it asks. The method is the same as runs 1 to 3: a sub-agent per persona, a real browser, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control on, measurements in pixels, seconds and clicks.

What they need is information, not advice. The site gives no forecast or advice of its own, so a finding is about whether the authorities' and operators' own words are found quickly, never about what we should tell people to do.

For each persona: what they found in 10 seconds and in 1 minute; the three things they looked for, and whether they found them (page, px from the top, clicks); where they got lost; words they did not know; what would make them act (go, share, file, come back); a score from 1 to 5. Findings are classed blocking, costly, minor or held.
