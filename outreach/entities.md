# "We have a page for you": a short note to every body with a page here

> Kept by incident.envoy@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 11:47 UTC. **A draft for Dinis Cruz. Nothing has been sent; a person sends every message**, from their own address, after reading it. From his words of about 11:45 UTC ([the brief](../BRIEF.md)): "we need to start reaching out to these entities and tell them we have a page for them ... we're happy to help ... if you want to collaborate ... any corrections." No contact details are kept here: each body is reached through the press or contact page on its own website, which the sender looks up. Bodies and public roles only. Not a warning service: follow [IPMA](https://www.ipma.pt/) and [ANEPC](https://prociv.gov.pt/).

## Before anything is sent

- **Not during the storm.** Bodies with an operational role (marked below) are written to only after IPMA's warnings for their area have ended. The others can go when Dinis says.
- **Read the body's page first.** The sender opens the page the note links to and checks it still says what the note says.
- **The reply route.** The note points to the press section and to the site's contact form. The form is being built by incident.desk@; until it is live, the note goes out with the sender's own address as the reply route, or waits.
- **Web Summit's organisers:** a person looks up their media route; we do not read their site by machine.

## The note (one template)

`[…]` is filled in by the sender. Short on purpose: it has to be read by a busy press office.

Subject: Storm Charles: `[body]` has a page in a public record of the storm; corrections welcome

> Hello,
>
> I am Dinis Cruz. With a team of AI agents that I direct, I keep a public record of Storm Charles in Lisbon: every warning and decision, each with its source and the time it was read. It is independent, it is one of the sources of PrompTugal.news, and it is not a warning service: every page sends readers to IPMA and ANEPC.
>
> `[body]` has a page in it: `[link to the body's page]`. It lists what `[body]` has said in public about the storm, each item with a link to your own notice.
>
> Three things, none of them urgent:
>
> 1. **Corrections.** If anything on that page is wrong, late or missing, tell us and we correct it in the open, with credit as you prefer.
> 2. **Your own notices.** If there is a public page or feed where you publish first, tell us and we read that, so the record cites you and not a report about you.
> 3. **Working together.** The data is open and free to reuse. If it can be of use to you, now or for the next event, we are happy to help.
>
> For journalists and press offices: `[link to the press section]`. To reach us: `[the contact form's address, or the sender's own]`.
>
> Thank you,
> Dinis Cruz

A Portuguese version is to be written once the English is agreed: most of these bodies will be written to in Portuguese.

## What a reply changes

As in [the outreach index](README.md): a reply is published only as its sender agrees; an on-the-record answer from the body that decides can turn evidence into fact, cited with its date; replies are not stored in this repository unless public.
