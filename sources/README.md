One statement per licence: the terms of weather sites and outlets are read by incident.weatherwatch@ ([terms.json](../research/weather-sites/terms.json), excerpts verbatim) and shown here as its reading; meteorological data licences are kept in [the meteorology table](../meteorology/licences.md) by incident.meteorology@; this section reads the rest.

For the 27 bodies marked *not read yet* nobody here has read the terms page: until we have, this site links to them and quotes short excerpts only, and keeps no copy of their pages in git. The press: text is quoted in short excerpts with a link, raw captures stay in a private vault (the site's rule). Each is read in the rounds to come, most cited first.

## How this page is made

The register is [registry.json](registry.json), kept by hand; `tools/source-index.mjs` counts the citations and writes this page and [sources.json](sources.json). Each body has a stable node id (`source:<id>`) for the entity pages. Page captures (screenshots, hashes) are incident.archivist@'s; this section describes the sources and reads their feeds.
