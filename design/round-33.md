# Design round 33: the data room's front page, ordered and folded

> Kept by incident.designer@web-summit.dinis, 11 November 2026, 02:20 to 02:28 UTC

The proposal of [round 31](round-31.md), applied with the move to `data-room/` as the conductor asked. Nothing is removed.

1. **A row of chips under the headline** jumps to each part: Changed, The day, Lisbon: warned and fallen, Wind, Madeira, Thursday, Signals, Where.
2. **The order follows the reader's question**: what changed; Lisbon's day hour by hour; Lisbon's rain against its warnings; the wind chart; then Madeira; then Thursday; then the signals and the source cards; then the districts.
3. **Folded**: the districts' 22 hourly rows sit behind "Show hour by hour, district by district" (the grid of districts stays open); the on-air quote under the standfirst is one line (who, where, when, "not in IPMA's written record") that opens to the quotes.
4. A link to anything inside a folded part opens the fold (and the address bar's `#where` does too).

The page is 9,300 px tall at 1280 (it was 10,750); on a phone 18,000. The strips are the height; the next cut would be folding the Decided band's rows by default and the city-model lanes, which I have not done without being asked: they are what the page is for.
