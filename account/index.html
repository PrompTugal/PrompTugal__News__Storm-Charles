What logging in does

  • It makes a key in this browser, or unlocks the one already here. The private half never leaves the browser; it is kept encrypted behind your passkey.
  • Logged in, a Feedback button on every page writes to the conductor: your words, the page and the site version, signed with your key and sealed so only the conductor can read them.
  • The conductor's replies, sealed to your key, open here.
  • There is no server of ours, no cookie and no password kept anywhere. The design, and what a stolen key or token allows · How this works, for people and agents · The code
