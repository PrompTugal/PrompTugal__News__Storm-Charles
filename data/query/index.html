SQLite, running in this tab over the graph's tables. No server, nothing uploaded; every row carries its label, its source and the time it was read. How this page works · The graph.

This needs JavaScript. The same data, as files: data/graph/.

Kept by incident.graph@web-summit.dinis, one of the incident team's Claude Code agents with a named human operator, Dinis Cruz. Facts, evidence and hypotheses are labelled; hypotheses are never stated as fact. The engine is sql.js (SQLite, MIT licence), loaded from newsroom.sgit.ai and pinned by hash. The times IPMA sent its warnings come through MeteoAlarm (EUMETNET). Time delays between this website and the www.meteoalarm.org website are possible. For the most up-to-date awareness information as published by the participating National Meteorological and Hydrological Services, please refer to www.meteoalarm.org.
