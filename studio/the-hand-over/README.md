## What this simplifies

- The blocks in chapter 3 are not to scale and the hours were not equal. The measured figures beside them are the real proportions: about 84,000 tokens at the start, 534,624 a call at the end.
- "Tokens per call" compares an old session's last hour with a new session's first calls. That is the fairest moment for the new one: it grows again from its first call. The two youngest (graph and studio, 9 and 8 calls) will look worse within the hour. The accountant's figure for when to hand over again is an estimate.
- Tokens are not money. Context read again from a cache is charged at a lower rate than new text; how much lower is the provider's business and is not stated here. Weighted at a tenth (an estimate), re-reading was still 89% of the team's cost.
- Less to read is not the same as work as good. The pack was tested on eight questions about the rules, one run of the whole files and two of the pack, marked by the accountant: not a test of doing the job. The new sessions' rounds passed the gate; nobody has compared the quality of their work with the old sessions'. The team's own policy says a saving that loses quality is not a saving.
- A hand-over loses things too. The note is its writer's judgement of what matters, as a summary is a model's; what was not written down is gone with the old session. The difference is that the note and the files can be read, corrected and added to. The librarian's first hand-over lived only in its status file and was not enough to carry on from; the Studio's second session found gaps as well and listed them in its status file.
- About 84,000 tokens of every first call is not touched by any of this: the harness, its tools and the repository's rules. The pack is about a sixth of that floor.
- Five agents is not the team. When this was read, 26 of 30 agents had measured a session at all, and the conductor's, the largest, had not handed over. A sixth agent (weatherwatch) had written its hand-over but its new session had no line yet.
- The times are from the files and the commits (UTC). Dinis's sentence is given as BRIEF.md has it, "close to his words", at about 02:50. incident.reporter@ had no trace of this episode when this was made (reporter/traces/index.json held six, none of it); when it has one, it is the fuller record.
